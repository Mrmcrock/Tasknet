Frequently asked questions.

Everything you need to know about TaskNet, finding tasks and getting started.

Getting started

What is TaskNet?

TaskNet is a marketplace concept designed to connect people who need tasks completed with people who can help.

How does TaskNet work?

People looking for help can browse task listings. People offering services can explore opportunities to help with suitable tasks. Booking and account features are still being developed.

Is TaskNet free to use?

TaskNet's pricing and any platform fees have not been finalised yet. Check the website for updates as development continues.

Accounts

How do I create an account?

Visit the Sign Up page to see the registration form. Account creation is not active yet because authentication has not been connected.

What if I forget my password?

Password recovery will be available once the account system has been implemented.

Can I update my profile?

Profile management is planned for a future stage of TaskNet's development.

Tasks and payments

How do I find a task?

Visit the Hire page to browse the example task listings. These are currently demonstrations and are not confirmed real jobs.

How do I post a task?

A task-posting feature is planned, but tasks cannot yet be published through a working backend.

How do payments work?

Payments have not been connected. Do not send money or share payment details through this demonstration website.

Safety and support

How does TaskNet keep people safe?

Safety procedures and platform safeguards still need to be implemented before real tasks can be arranged. Users should not treat the current demonstration as a verified service.

How can I report a problem?

Visit the Contact page for the planned contact form. It is not connected to a message service yet, so submissions will not reach TaskNet.

Where can I ask another question?

Visit our Contact page to see the contact options planned for TaskNet.
