Frequently asked questions

Here are a few things you might want to know.

What is TaskNet?

TaskNet is a marketplace concept designed to connect people who need tasks completed with people who can help.

How do I find a task?

Visit the Hire page to browse the sample tasks. Listings are currently demonstrations rather than confirmed real-world jobs.

Can I create an account?

You can explore the sign-up page, but account creation is not active yet because authentication has not been connected.

How do I report a safety concern?

Use the contact form to describe your concern once a working submission service is available. Until then, this demonstration cannot receive reports.
